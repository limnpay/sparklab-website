### 4. How to Get Started — 5-step process
1. **Express Interest** — Fill out our interest form or contact us directly. We'll schedule a call to learn about your school and answer questions.
2. **Plan Together** — We'll work with you to determine the best timing, which classes to involve, and how to structure the program for your school.
3. **Teacher Training** — We provide a brief orientation for facilitating teachers so they feel confident running the program.
4. **Launch & Support** — Students begin the 8-week program. We're available throughout for questions and support.
5. **Competition Day** — Students present to judges, winners are announced, and prizes are awarded. We help coordinate the event.

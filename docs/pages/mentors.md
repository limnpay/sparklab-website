### 2. Two Ways to Get Involved — side-by-side cards
- **Become a Judge** (Award icon)
  - "Evaluate student presentations and help select winners on competition day."
  - What you'll do: Assess business ideas, marketing, and financials · Provide constructive feedback to teams · Help select winning teams using scoring criteria · Attend the final competition event
  - Time commitment: 1-2 days during competition
- **Become a Mentor** (Users icon)
  - "Guide students throughout the program with your expertise and experience."
  - What you'll do: Share industry insights and career advice · Help students refine their business concepts · Provide guidance on presentations and pitching · Inspire students with your professional journey
  - Time commitment: 2-4 hours per month

### 4. What We're Looking For
- "You don't need to be a CEO or have decades of experience. We're looking for professionals who are:"

  - Experienced in business, marketing, finance, or related fields
  - Good communicators who can explain concepts clearly
  - Patient and encouraging with students
  - Reliable and committed to their volunteer role

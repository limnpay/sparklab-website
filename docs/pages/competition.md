## Per-page issues
- Winning teams are anonymous: no team name, no school, no business idea, no quote, no founder photo captions. Visitors learn nothing about what actually won.
- The intentional removal of prize amounts here contradicts the Home page (`$15K+`, `$7,500`, `$5,000`, `$2,500`).
- Two of the three winner card photos use shots of multiple students with no indication of which team/business; the third shows one student. No coverage of runner-up teams, no judges' names, no event venue or date.
- "Inaugural" implies first year — there is no historical record, no comparison to previous cohorts, and no clue about cadence (annual? semester?).
